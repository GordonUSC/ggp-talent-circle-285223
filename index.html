Candidate experience, as we plan to practice it

  1. Prepare before the room. Every Scholar gets a resume or portfolio review before meeting a recruiter.
  2. Consent both ways. We ask the Scholar and the recruiter before any intro.
  3. Everyone hears back. A clear answer within two weeks, yes or no.
  4. No with a next door. Every no comes with one concrete next step.
  5. Respect recruiters' time. One clear ask per season, sized to what they can say yes to.
